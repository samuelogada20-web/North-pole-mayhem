# NORTH POLE MAYHEM: LAGOS NIGHT

> It's Christmas Eve in Lagos, and for the first time ever, it's snowing.

**NORTH POLE MAYHEM: LAGOS NIGHT** is a browser-based 3D action-adventure for
up to many players at once. Five rival couriers race through a stylized Lagos
night to deliver the legendary Golden Parcel to Tarkwa Bay by midnight, while
something in the shadows watches every move.

Part 1 of a planned series.

## The Setting
Warm harmattan haze, hundreds of string lights, yellow danfo buses, loud
generators, and power cuts that plunge the city dark before the lights roar
back. Then the snow begins to fall, piling up on the streets, slowing your
steps and keeping every footprint you leave behind.

## The Characters
- **Boss Bauble**: a tiny baby executive. Ability: *Executive Order* freezes
  nearby players.
- **Jingle Rapper**: laid-back, with bells that follow him. Ability: *Bass Drop*
  sends out a shockwave.
- **Harlequin Holly**: a playful jester in pink and blue. Ability: *Snowball
  Barrage*.
- **Mr. Mistle-Joke**: a trickster in a purple coat. Ability: *Surprise Gift*
  traps.
- **Tinsel Tola**: a street-smart elf courier on a glowing okada. Ability:
  *Shortcut*, a golden-trail dash.
- **???**: a tall black silhouette with two glowing eyes. He never speaks. He
  only whispers one word.

## Highlights
- Live multiplayer with chat, emotes, a leaderboard and a shared Christmas
  Spirit meter
- Cinematic intro and story cutscenes with a whispered narrator and subtitles
- Procedural Afrobeat-flavoured music and 3D positional sound effects
- Dynamic snow, footprints, day-to-night cycle and aurora
- Fully rebindable controls, gamepad support and mobile touch controls
- Low, Medium and High quality settings for mid-range phones

Built with **Three.js**, **Python (FastAPI + WebSockets)** and the Web Audio
and Web Speech APIs. No build step: one HTML file and one Python server.
